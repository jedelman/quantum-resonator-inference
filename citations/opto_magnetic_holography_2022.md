# Dynamic Complex Opto-Magnetic Holography
**Source:** Nature Communications (2022)
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-35023-9
**Authors:** Makowski M., Stupiakiewicz A., et al.
**Institution:** Warsaw University of Technology; Chiba University

## Relevance to Project
LOW. Computer-generated holography using ultrafast laser writing on GdFeCo ferrimagnetic film. All-optical switching (AOS) enables point-by-point hologram writing at picosecond speeds.

## Why Low Relevance
- This is holographic display technology, not inference
- Binary amplitude modulation (not the analog weights we need)
- No connection to token inference or resonator computing
- Material (GdFeCo) not on our materials shortlist

## One Useful Observation
Detour-phase encoding (Lohmann method) enables complex modulation (amplitude + phase) from a binary medium. Could be relevant if we consider binary-weight holographic resonators. But this is a distant connection — note only.
